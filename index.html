Turn the mirrors so light from the emitter reaches the glowing receptor.

Campaign

How to play

  1. Click a mirror to flip it between / and \.
  2. Light leaves the emitter and bounces off mirrors at right angles.
  3. Route the beam into the receptor on the far side to lock it.
  4. Campaign: 1000 puzzles. Every level is harder than the one before: more decoy mirrors, longer routes, bigger boards and walls. Stars reward the fewest moves.
  5. Hard Mode: 6 larger puzzles with locked mirrors, a countdown, and a move limit of the minimum plus one.
